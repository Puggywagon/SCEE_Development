## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Computing interactions between every pair scales as N² — expensive. Cutoffs are the practical solution.

Short range interactions like LJ fall off steeply — beyond some cutoff radius the interaction is negligible. Two standard remedies for the discontinuity at cutoff:
- Potential truncation and shifting — shift potential to go smoothly to zero at cutoff
- Potential switching — smooth switching function to taper potential to zero

For longer range interactions (electrostatics) a simple cutoff is not good enough — Ewald summation required.
