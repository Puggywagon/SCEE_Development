## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Your simulation box is tiled infinitely in all directions — an infinite lattice of identical copies. When a molecule drifts through one face it reappears on the opposite face with the same velocity. No walls, no surfaces, every molecule sees a bulk-like environment.

**Minimum image convention:** When computing interactions between two molecules, only consider the nearest periodic image of each pair. This keeps the interaction calculation tractable and physically sensible.

Key constraint: your cutoff distance must always be smaller than half the box length.
