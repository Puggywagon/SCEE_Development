## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Both methods sample thermodynamically relevant regions of configuration space. The difference is how.

**MD:** Follows physically real time evolution. Newton's laws. Trajectory has genuine dynamical meaning — transport properties accessible. Constrained to physically realistic moves — can struggle with high barriers.

**MC:** Abandons physical time evolution for purely statistical sampling. Trial moves proposed and accepted/rejected. Moves can be large and creative. Loses all dynamical information — equilibrium properties only.

**For this thesis:**
- SCEE project → MD. Need time-ordered trajectory of liquid configurations representative of thermal motion. Dynamical continuity ensures physically realistic sampling.
- Zeno line project → MC (FEASST). Purely equilibrium thermodynamic properties. MC flexibility in move types and ensemble sampling well suited to mapping thermodynamic behaviour across wide range of state points.
