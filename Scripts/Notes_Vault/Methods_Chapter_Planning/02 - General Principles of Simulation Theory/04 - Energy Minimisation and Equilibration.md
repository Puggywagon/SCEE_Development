## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Before collecting meaningful data the system must be in a physically sensible state.

**Energy minimisation:** Moves atoms downhill on the potential energy surface until a local minimum is reached. Not a dynamical process — no temperature or time. Common algorithms: steepest descent and conjugate gradient.

**Equilibration:** Dynamical process — run simulation without collecting data, allowing system to relax to proper thermodynamic equilibrium. Monitor energy, density, pressure until they stabilise and fluctuate around a steady mean. Only then begin production run.

The distinction between equilibration and production is one of the most practically important concepts in simulation.
