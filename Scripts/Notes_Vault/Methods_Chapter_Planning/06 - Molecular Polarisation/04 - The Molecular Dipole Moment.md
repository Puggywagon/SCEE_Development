## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

The dipole moment is a vector quantity measuring the separation of positive and negative charge within a molecule. For point charges: sum over all charges of charge multiplied by position vector. For continuous electron density: expectation value of position operator weighted by charge density.

Units: Debye. Water gas phase ~1.85 D. Ethanol gas phase ~1.69 D.

**Key points:**
- Origin dependent for charged molecules — well defined for neutral molecules
- Quantum mechanical observable — requires knowledge of electron density to compute exactly
- Experimentally accessible in gas phase via microwave spectroscopy and dielectric measurements

Gas phase: computed from QC calculation on isolated molecule. Condensed phase: requires full SCEE machinery. Difference between the two = direct measure of polarisation enhancement.
