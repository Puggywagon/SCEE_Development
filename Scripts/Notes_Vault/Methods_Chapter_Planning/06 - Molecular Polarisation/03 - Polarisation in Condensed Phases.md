## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

In gas phase: molecules well separated, small polarisation. In condensed phase: every molecule surrounded by neighbours at close range. Local electric field is superposition of contributions from all neighbours — can be very large.

**Consequences:**
- Molecule in liquid continuously polarised by environment
- Instantaneous dipole moment larger than gas phase value
- Enhancement can be 20-50% for polar molecules (water, methanol, ethanol)

**Self-consistency problem:** A polarises B, B's resulting dipole feeds back into field at A, changes A's polarisation, changes field at B... The true polarisation state is the solution to a many-body self-consistency problem.

Fixed charge force fields cannot capture this — they assign a single charge to each atom that never changes. This is the motivation for SCEE.
