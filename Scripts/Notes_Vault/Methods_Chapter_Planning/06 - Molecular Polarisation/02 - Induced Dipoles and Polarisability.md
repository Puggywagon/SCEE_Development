## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

An applied electric field displaces electron density, creating an **induced dipole moment**. The molecular property determining how easily this occurs is **polarisability** (alpha).

For small fields: induced dipole moment proportional to applied field, polarisability as proportionality constant. Units of volume. Large diffuse electron clouds (heavier atoms, aromatic systems) more polarisable.

In general: polarisability is a tensor — response may differ along different molecular axes. Anisotropy affects optical properties and Raman spectra.

Connection to London dispersion: the r⁻⁶ attractive term in LJ captures dispersion arising from correlated fluctuations in induced dipoles. More polarisable molecules have stronger dispersion — why LJ epsilon increases with molecular size.
