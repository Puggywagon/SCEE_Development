## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Polarisation is the displacement of electron density within a molecule in response to an electric field.

**Three main sources:**
- **Electronic polarisation:** Electron cloud distorts in response to applied field. Essentially instantaneous. Present in every molecule.
- **Ionic/atomic polarisation:** Displacement of nuclei relative to each other — bond stretching. Slower than electronic. Still much faster than molecular reorientation.
- **Orientational polarisation:** Reorientation of permanent dipoles in response to field. Slowest. Strongly temperature dependent.

For molecular simulation: electronic polarisation most relevant — operates on timescales of molecular motion, directly affects instantaneous dipole moment.
