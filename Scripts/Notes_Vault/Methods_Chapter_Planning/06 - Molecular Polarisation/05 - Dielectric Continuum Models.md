## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Replace molecular environment with uniform polarisable medium characterised by the **dielectric constant** (relative permittivity) epsilon.

Molecule in cavity carved from dielectric medium. Medium develops **reaction field** — electric field acting back on molecule stabilising its charge distribution.

**Onsager reaction field model** and **polarisable continuum model (PCM):** Standard continuum approaches in quantum chemistry.

**Advantages:** Computationally cheap, captures average electrostatic effect of solvent reasonably well.

**Limitations:** Misses all molecular detail — no specific hydrogen bonds, no local structure, no fluctuations in local field.

For SCEE work: continuum models represent important point of comparison. They are the simplest way to account for condensed phase polarisation effects — understanding their limitations motivates the explicit solvent QM/MM approach.
