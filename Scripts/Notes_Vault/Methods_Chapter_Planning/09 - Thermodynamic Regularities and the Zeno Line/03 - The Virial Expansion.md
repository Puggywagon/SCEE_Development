## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

At low to moderate densities, equation of state expressed as power series expansion in density. Z written as 1 + series of density-dependent correction terms. Each successive term captures interactions between increasingly large clusters of molecules. Coefficients (B2, B3, B4...) temperature dependent, encode information about intermolecular potential.

**Second virial coefficient B2:** Captures pairwise interactions. Negative at low T (attractions dominate), positive at high T (repulsions dominate).

Provides analytical route to characterising Zeno line at low densities directly from intermolecular potential without simulations. The slope and curvature of Zeno line near zero density can be derived analytically from virial coefficients.

For square-well fluids: exact analytical expressions for B2 and B3. For Mie fluids: virial coefficients computed numerically via Mayer sampling MC.
