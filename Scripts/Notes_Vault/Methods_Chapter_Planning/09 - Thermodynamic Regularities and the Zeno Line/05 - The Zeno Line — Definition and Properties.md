## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Locus of points in temperature-density plane where Z = 1. Name from Zeno of Elea.

**Key empirical observation:** For broad range of real fluids (noble gases, simple diatomics, small nonpolar molecules) Zeno line is very nearly straight. Extends from Boyle temperature at zero density down to near triple point where it meets liquid-solid freezing curve.

**Model fluids:** Neither LJ, square-well, nor Mie n-6 fluids produce perfectly straight Zeno line. Curved, deviating particularly at high densities/low temperatures.

Degree and direction of curvature depend sensitively on potential parameters — repulsive exponent n for Mie, well width λ for square-well. Finding parameter values minimising curvature is key goal.

Connection to phase behaviour: fluids with straighter Zeno lines show better agreement with VLE curves of real corresponding states fluids. Zeno line is sensitive diagnostic of overall model quality across full phase diagram.
