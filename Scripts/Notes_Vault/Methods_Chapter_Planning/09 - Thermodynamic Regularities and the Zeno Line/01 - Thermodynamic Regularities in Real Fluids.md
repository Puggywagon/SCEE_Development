## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Despite complexity of intermolecular forces, thermodynamic properties of pure fluids exhibit elegant regularities suggesting underlying simplicity transcending molecular details.

**Law of corresponding states (van der Waals):** Thermodynamic properties of many fluids collapse onto universal curve when scaled by critical properties. Corresponding states fluids: noble gases, simple diatomics, small nonpolar molecules.

**Law of rectilinear diameter:** Mean of coexisting vapour and liquid densities varies linearly with temperature along VLE curve. Empirical but holds remarkably well.

**Zeno line:** Another member of this family. Nearly linear for broad range of real fluids.

Deeper reason for regularities not fully understood but renormalization group framework provides compelling argument — real fluids lie on same RG trajectory, large scale behaviour governed by same underlying fixed point. Simple pair potentials don't generally lie on these trajectories.
