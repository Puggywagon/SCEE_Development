## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Liquid branch of VLE curve never crosses Zeno line — Zeno line acts as boundary that coexistence curve approaches but does not cross. Based on thermodynamic arguments: Zeno line should become tangent to extension of liquid branch at zero temperature.

**Shape constrains shape:** Zeno line shape constrains coexistence curve shape. Straight Zeno line associated with coexistence curve most closely resembling real corresponding states fluids.

From group's paper: square-well and Mie fluids with straightest Zeno lines show closest agreement with VLE envelope of real corresponding states fluids. Non-trivial result — Zeno line connected to overall thermodynamic consistency of molecular model.

**Practical implication:** Computing both Zeno line and VLE from MC simulations — assess model fluids against two complementary features simultaneously. Multicanonical MC + histogram reweighting samples both phases efficiently and gives direct access to coexistence conditions.
