## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Temperature at which second virial coefficient passes through zero. Attractive and repulsive contributions to pairwise interactions exactly cancel at level of B2. Fluid behaves as if ideal in the low density limit.

Natural reference temperature for Zeno line — defines one endpoint. At zero density, Zeno line intersects temperature axis exactly at Boyle temperature.

**For Mie n-6:** Boyle temperature decreases with increasing n — stiffer repulsive cores require lower temperatures for attractive/repulsive balance.

**For square-well:** Boyle temperature increases with well width λ — greater attractive interaction volume for wider wells.

Boyle temperature + Zeno density define natural scaling for Zeno line. When T and ρ scaled by these quantities, corresponding states fluid Zeno lines collapse onto universal 45° straight line.
