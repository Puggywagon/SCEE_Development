## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Principle of corresponding states: thermodynamic properties of fluids in same corresponding states class are identical when expressed in reduced units scaled by critical properties.

When Zeno line scaled by Boyle temperature and Zeno density: corresponding states fluids collapse onto universal 45° straight line. Boyle scaling more fundamental than critical scaling for Zeno line — Boyle T and density computable directly from virial coefficients without locating critical point.

Failure of simple model potentials to reproduce straight Zeno line: these potentials don't lie on same RG trajectory as real corresponding states fluids.

**For project:** Systematic variation of Mie exponents and examination of effect on Zeno line = searching for region of parameter space where model potential best approximates RG trajectory of real corresponding states fluids.
