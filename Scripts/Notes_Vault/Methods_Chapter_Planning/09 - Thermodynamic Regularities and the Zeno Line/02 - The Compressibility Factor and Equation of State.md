## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Z defined as ratio of actual molar volume to molar volume of ideal gas at same T and P. For ideal gas Z = 1 by definition.

**Deviations from unity:**
- Low density/high T: nearly ideal, Z ~ 1
- Moderate density: attractive interactions dominate, Z < 1
- Very high density: repulsive core dominates, Z > 1

Equation of state: functional relationship between P, T, and density (or Z, T, density).

Van der Waals EOS: simplest model capturing both repulsive and attractive contributions, predicts VLE transition. Predicts perfectly straight Zeno line — motivated early theoretical interest.

For simulation work: Z is the central quantity computed. At each state point evaluate Z from MC simulation. Zeno line = locus of state points where Z = 1.
