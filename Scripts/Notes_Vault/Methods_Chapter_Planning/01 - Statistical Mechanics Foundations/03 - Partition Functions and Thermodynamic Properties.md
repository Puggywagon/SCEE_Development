## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

The partition function encodes everything about the probability distribution over microstates. For the canonical ensemble it is written as **Z** (or **Q**) — a weighted sum over all microstates where each microstate is weighted by the Boltzmann factor.

The partition function might look like just a normalisation constant, but it is extraordinarily powerful — virtually every thermodynamic quantity can be derived from it by taking appropriate derivatives:
- Helmholtz free energy A
- Pressure P
- Internal energy U
- Entropy S
- Heat capacity Cv

This is why statistical mechanics is so elegant — if you know Z, you know everything about the equilibrium thermodynamics of your system.
