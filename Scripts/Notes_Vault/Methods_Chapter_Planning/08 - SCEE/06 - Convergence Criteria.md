## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Two distinct types of convergence — important to distinguish clearly.

**Inner loop convergence:** Self-consistency of charges within single configuration. Monitor change in ESP charges between successive iterations — converged when change falls below defined threshold. Balance between accuracy and computational cost.

**Outer loop convergence:** Statistical convergence of ensemble average dipole moment with respect to number of configurations. Running average should stop drifting, statistical uncertainty should decrease. Gives confidence final reported dipole moment is true ensemble average.

Both should be reported and discussed in results chapter as part of methodology validation.
