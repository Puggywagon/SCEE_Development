## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Standard electrostatic embedding: QM wavefunction responds to MM point charges — improvement over mechanical embedding. But MM charges typically derived from gas phase QC calculations — represent charge distribution of molecule in vacuum, not liquid.

In condensed phase: molecules significantly more polarised than in gas phase. MM charges don't accurately represent true electrostatic environment. QM calculation performed in environment inconsistent with real liquid state.

Enhancement 20-50% above gas phase for polar molecules — not a small correction. Using gas phase charges systematically underestimates electric field experienced by QM molecule. Result: dipole moment intermediate between gas phase and true liquid values.

**Self-consistency is the solution:** Iterate — update MM charges to reflect polarised liquid environment, repeat QM/MM calculation until charges and wavefunction are mutually consistent.
