## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

**Direct validation:** Compare with experiment. Gas phase dipole moments well established from microwave spectroscopy. SCEE condensed phase values compared against dielectric measurements and other computational approaches.

**Internal consistency checks:**
- Convergence with respect to MM region size
- Convergence with respect to number of configurations
- Sensitivity to QM method and basis set choice

**Sources of systematic error:**
- MM force field used to generate MD configurations — poor liquid structure → unrepresentative configurations
- Choice of charge fitting scheme (ESP vs RESP)
- Neglect of explicit polarisation in MM region — distinguishes SCEE from full polarisable embedding

Understanding and quantifying these sources of error is an important part of the scientific contribution.
