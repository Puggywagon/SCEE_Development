# Equation — Virial Expansion of the Equation of State

**Topic:** See connections below

## Equation

$$
Z = \frac{\beta p}{\rho} = 1 + B_2(T)\rho + B_3(T)\rho^2 + B_4(T)\rho^3 + \cdots
$$

---

## Term Breakdown

| Symbol | Meaning | Units |
|--------|---------|-------|
| $Z$ | Compressibility factor | Dimensionless |
| $p$ | Pressure | Pa |
| $\beta$ | Inverse temperature $1/k_BT$ | J⁻¹ |
| $\rho$ | Number density $N/V$ | m⁻³ or reduced units |
| $B_2(T)$ | Second virial coefficient — pairwise interactions | m³ or reduced units |
| $B_3(T)$ | Third virial coefficient — three-body cluster interactions | m⁶ or reduced units |

---

## Physical Interpretation

Power series expansion of the equation of state in density. Each successive term captures interactions between increasingly large clusters of molecules. Virial coefficients are temperature dependent and encode information about the intermolecular potential. Provides analytical route to characterising Zeno line properties at low densities directly from the potential. Central to the Zeno line project.
