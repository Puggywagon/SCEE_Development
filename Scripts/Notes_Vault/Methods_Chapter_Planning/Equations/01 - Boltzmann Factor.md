**Topic:** See connections below
**Tags:** #statmech #equation
**Status:** #to-verify

---

## Equation

$$
e^{-\beta E_i} = e^{-E_i / k_B T}
$$

---

## Term Breakdown

| Symbol | Meaning | Units |
|--------|---------|-------|
| $E_i$ | Energy of microstate i | J or reduced units |
| $k_B$ | Boltzmann constant | J K⁻¹ |
| $T$ | Temperature | K |
| $\beta$ | Inverse temperature, $\beta = 1/k_BT$ | J⁻¹ |

---

## Physical Interpretation

The probability weighting of a microstate in the canonical ensemble. States with lower energy are exponentially more probable at a given temperature. The Boltzmann factor is the fundamental building block of statistical mechanics.
