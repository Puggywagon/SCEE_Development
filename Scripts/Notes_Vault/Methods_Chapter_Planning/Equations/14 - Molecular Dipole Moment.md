## Equation

$$
\boldsymbol{\mu} = \sum_i q_i \mathbf{r}_i \quad \text{(point charges)} \qquad \boldsymbol{\mu} = -e \int \mathbf{r} \, \rho(\mathbf{r}) \, d\mathbf{r} \quad \text{(continuous)}
$$

---

## Term Breakdown

| Symbol | Meaning | Units |
|--------|---------|-------|
| $\boldsymbol{\mu}$ | Molecular dipole moment vector | C m or Debye |
| $q_i$ | Charge on atom i | C |
| $\mathbf{r}_i$ | Position vector of atom i | m |
| $e$ | Elementary charge | C |
| $\rho(\mathbf{r})$ | Electron density as function of position | e m⁻³ |
| 1 Debye | = 3.336 × 10⁻³⁰ C m | Unit conversion |

---

## Physical Interpretation

Vector quantity measuring separation of positive and negative charge within a molecule. For point charges: sum of charge times position. For continuous electron density (real QM description): expectation value of position operator weighted by charge density. Water gas phase ~1.85 D. Ethanol gas phase ~1.69 D. Condensed phase values significantly enhanced by polarisation.
