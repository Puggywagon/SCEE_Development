# Equation — Boltzmann Entropy Formula

**Topic:** See connections below
**Tags:** #statmech #equation
**Status:** #to-verify

---

## Equation

$$
S = k_B \ln W
$$

---

## Term Breakdown

| Symbol | Meaning | Units |
|--------|---------|-------|
| $S$ | Entropy | J K⁻¹ |
| $k_B$ | Boltzmann constant | J K⁻¹ |
| $W$ | Number of accessible microstates (multiplicity) | Dimensionless |

---

## Physical Interpretation

Entropy is proportional to the logarithm of the number of accessible microstates. More accessible microstates = higher entropy. This equation is engraved on Boltzmann's tombstone in Vienna. It connects the macroscopic concept of entropy to the microscopic count of states.
