## Equation

$$
Z = \frac{pV}{Nk_BT} = \frac{p}{\rho k_B T}
$$

---

## Term Breakdown

| Symbol | Meaning | Units |
|--------|---------|-------|
| $Z$ | Compressibility factor | Dimensionless |
| $p$ | Pressure | Pa |
| $V$ | Volume | m³ |
| $N$ | Number of particles | Dimensionless |
| $k_B$ | Boltzmann constant | J K⁻¹ |
| $T$ | Temperature | K |
| $\rho$ | Number density $N/V$ | m⁻³ |

---

## Physical Interpretation

Ratio of actual molar volume to ideal gas molar volume at same T and P. Z = 1 for ideal gas. Z < 1 when attractive interactions dominate (moderate density). Z > 1 when repulsive core dominates (very high density). The Zeno line is the locus of state points where Z = 1.
