## Equation

$$
A = -k_B T \ln Z \qquad P = k_B T \left(\frac{\partial \ln Z}{\partial V}\right)_{N,T} \qquad U = -\left(\frac{\partial \ln Z}{\partial \beta}\right)_{N,V}
$$

---

## Term Breakdown

| Symbol | Meaning | Units |
|--------|---------|-------|
| $A$ | Helmholtz free energy | J |
| $P$ | Pressure | Pa |
| $U$ | Internal energy | J |
| $Z$ | Canonical partition function | Dimensionless |
| $k_B$ | Boltzmann constant | J K⁻¹ |
| $T$ | Temperature | K |
| $V$ | Volume | m³ |
| $\beta$ | Inverse temperature $1/k_BT$ | J⁻¹ |

---

## Physical Interpretation

The bridge equations connect the partition function to macroscopic thermodynamic quantities. If you know Z as a function of N, V, T you can derive any equilibrium thermodynamic property by differentiation. This is the power of the partition function.
