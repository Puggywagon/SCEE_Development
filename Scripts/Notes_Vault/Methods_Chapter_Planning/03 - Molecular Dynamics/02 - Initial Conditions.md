## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

**Initial positions:** Typically placed on regular lattice (often FCC) at correct bulk density for target state point. Avoids catastrophic atomic overlaps.

**Initial velocities:** Assigned randomly from Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution at target temperature. Follows from equipartition theorem — each quadratic degree of freedom carries average kinetic energy ½k_BT. Apply correction to ensure total linear momentum is exactly zero.

Connection to statistical mechanics: assigning Maxwell-Boltzmann velocities is sampling the kinetic energy part of the canonical partition function.
