## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

**All atom (AA):** Interaction sites on every atom including hydrogens. Most detailed.

**United atom (UA):** Absorbs hydrogen atoms into bonded carbon or oxygen. CH₂ group as single site. Computationally faster — fewer pair interactions.

Works well for alkanes and alcohols — hydrogens shielded by heavier atoms, small individual contribution to electrostatics.

**For ethanol SCEE work:** UA model treats CH₃ and CH₂ groups as single sites — simplifies simulation. But the QM/MM treatment uses the real all atom electronic structure — UA model used for configuration generation, not the quantum calculation.
