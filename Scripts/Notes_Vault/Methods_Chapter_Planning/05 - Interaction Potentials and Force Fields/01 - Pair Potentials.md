## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Pair potentials assume total energy can be written as sum over all pairs of atoms.

**Lennard-Jones (LJ):** Captures Pauli repulsion (r⁻¹²) and London dispersion attraction (r⁻⁶). Two parameters: epsilon (well depth, interaction strength) and sigma (collision diameter, length scale). Reference model for noble gases and simple molecules.

**Mie potential:** Generalisation of LJ with adjustable repulsive and attractive exponents. Better reproduces real fluid properties. Increasingly common in modern force fields for engineering applications. Directly relevant to Zeno line project.

**Square-well potential:** Hard core repulsion + constant attractive well of fixed width and depth. Analytically tractable. Captures essential physics in simplest possible form. Also relevant to Zeno line project.
