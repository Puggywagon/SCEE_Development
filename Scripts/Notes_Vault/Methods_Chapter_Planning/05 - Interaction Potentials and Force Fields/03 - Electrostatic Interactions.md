## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Polar molecules carry partial charges that interact via Coulomb's law. Electrostatic interactions decay only as r⁻¹ — long ranged, cannot simply be truncated.

Point charge models: partial charges on atomic sites interact via Coulomb's law.

**Ewald summation:** Splits electrostatic interaction into short range part (real space) and long range part (reciprocal space via Fourier transform). Together gives exact electrostatic energy of infinite periodic system.

**Particle mesh Ewald (PME):** Computationally efficient Ewald implementation used in essentially all modern codes including GROMACS.
