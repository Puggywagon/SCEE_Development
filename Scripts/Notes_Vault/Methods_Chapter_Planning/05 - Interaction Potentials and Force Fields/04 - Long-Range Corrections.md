## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Truncating LJ at cutoff introduces systematic error — missing contributions from all pairs beyond cutoff. For homogeneous fluid, assume pair distribution function is unity beyond cutoff and add analytic tail corrections to energy and pressure.

Important for accurate thermodynamic properties, particularly pressure and equation of state. For Zeno line work — tail corrections are necessary for quantitatively accurate results.

For electrostatics: simple tail corrections insufficient due to slow decay — Ewald summation necessary.
