## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

A force field is a set of empirical parameters fitted to reproduce chosen target properties — typically combination of QC calculations and experimental data (liquid densities, vapour pressures, heats of vaporisation).

Force fields are models, not fundamental theories — they have a domain of validity. Fitted to reproduce liquid densities at ambient conditions may not transferably describe vapour phase or high pressure behaviour.

Key tension: accuracy vs computational cost. UA fixed charge models faster but approximate. Full polarisable force fields more accurate but expensive.

For mapping thermodynamic regularities over wide range of state points — well validated UA or AA fixed charge model may be adequate. For accurate dipole moments in condensed phase — full QM/MM treatment required (SCEE project).
