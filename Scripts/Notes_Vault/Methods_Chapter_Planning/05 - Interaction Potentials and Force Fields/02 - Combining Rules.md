## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Pair potentials parameterised for like-like interactions. Need unlike interactions (e.g. carbon-oxygen).

**Lorentz-Berthelot rules:**
- sigma for unlike pair = arithmetic mean of like sigmas
- epsilon for unlike pair = geometric mean of like epsilons

Empirical approximations — not derived from first principles. Deviations sometimes introduced as additional fitting parameters.
