## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Full QM treatment of molecule in solution — solving Schrödinger equation for entire system — computationally intractable. Classical force field misses electronic structure detail essential for properties like dipole moments.

**QM/MM:** System divided into QM region (small, chemically important, treated quantum mechanically) and MM region (surrounding environment, treated classically). Total energy = QM energy of inner region + MM energy of outer region + coupling term.

For SCEE: QM region = single solvent molecule. MM region = everything else. Well justified — dipole moment is a property of the central molecule, surrounding solvent well described classically.

Critical question: how is coupling between the two regions handled?
