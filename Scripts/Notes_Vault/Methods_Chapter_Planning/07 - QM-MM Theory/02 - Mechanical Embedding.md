## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Simplest QM/MM coupling. QM and MM regions interact purely through classical terms. QM calculation performed on isolated QM region in gas phase — no influence from MM environment on electronic structure.

**Limitation:** MM environment has no influence on QM wavefunction. Electronic structure identical to gas phase. No polarisation of QM region by environment. For dipole moments directly determined by electron density — fundamental deficiency.

Generally considered least accurate embedding scheme.
