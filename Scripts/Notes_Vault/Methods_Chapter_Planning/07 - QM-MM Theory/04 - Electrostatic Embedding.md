## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

MM partial charges included directly in QM Hamiltonian as one-electron operators — appear as external point charges that QM wavefunction responds to. QM electron density polarised by MM environment. Significant improvement over mechanical embedding.

**Key distinction from polarisable embedding:** MM charges are fixed — do not respond to QM charge density. Polarisation is one-directional: MM environment influences QM region, but QM region does not influence MM environment.

**The inconsistency SCEE addresses:** MM charges typically derived from gas phase calculations — don't reflect polarised liquid environment. SCEE iterates the electrostatic embedding calculation — updating MM charges to reflect polarised liquid environment and repeating until self-consistency achieved.
