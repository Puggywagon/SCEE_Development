## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Most sophisticated. MM region described by polarisable force field — MM atoms carry permanent charges AND polarisabilities. Induced dipoles respond to electric field from QM region and from each other.

Fully self-consistent treatment: QM electron density polarises MM environment, polarised MM environment feeds back into QM Hamiltonian, solved simultaneously to self-consistency.

**Price:** Complexity and computational cost. Polarisable force fields more expensive. Less widely available and validated than fixed charge force fields.

Theoretical gold standard for QM/MM. SCEE is an alternative route to achieving similar accuracy through a different and more practical route.
