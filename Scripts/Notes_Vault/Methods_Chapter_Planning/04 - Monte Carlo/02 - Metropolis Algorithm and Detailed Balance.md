## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

The acceptance criterion at the heart of virtually all MC simulations.

If proposed move lowers energy → always accepted. If raises energy → accepted with probability given by Boltzmann factor of energy difference. Uphill moves accepted with decreasing probability the larger the energy increase.

**Detailed balance:** For MC to correctly sample a given ensemble, the probability of being in any microstate must converge to the correct Boltzmann distribution. Detailed balance is the sufficient condition — rate of transitions from A to B equals rate from B to A at equilibrium. Metropolis criterion satisfies detailed balance.
