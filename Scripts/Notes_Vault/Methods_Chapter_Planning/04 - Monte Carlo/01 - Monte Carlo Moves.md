## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Rather than integrating equations of motion, MC generates a new configuration by proposing a random change — typically displacing a randomly chosen molecule by a random vector, or rotating it by a random angle. For NPT, volume moves also included.

Moves are entirely unphysical — no concept of force or acceleration. What controls acceptance is the Metropolis algorithm.

Efficiency depends heavily on move size and type. Too small → explores slowly. Too large → most moves rejected due to overlaps. Target acceptance rate ~20-50%.
