## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

A simulation at one state point only gives information about that state point. Histogram reweighting extracts information about nearby state points by reweighting the energy histogram.

Near phase transitions: reweighted energy or density distribution becomes bimodal — two peaks corresponding to two coexisting phases. Directly relevant to Zeno line work for characterising vapour-liquid coexistence accurately.

**WHAM (Weighted Histogram Analysis Method):** Combines histograms from multiple simulations at different state points for optimal coverage.
