## AI Refresher

> *Conceptual scaffold — starting point before reading. Expand and refine from textbooks.*

Move types determine the ensemble:
- NVT — displacement and rotation moves only
- NPT — displacement, rotation, and volume moves
- Grand canonical (μVT) — displacement moves plus insertion and deletion

For Zeno line project: choice of ensemble directly connected to what thermodynamic properties you want to compute. Mapping equation of state across wide range of densities and temperatures may require multiple ensembles or advanced sampling schemes.
